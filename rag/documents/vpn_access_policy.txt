VPN Access Policy

VPN access is granted to all full-time employees by default upon onboarding.

Contractors and part-time employees require manager approval before VPN access is granted. Approval requests must be submitted through the IT support portal.

VPN credentials must not be shared between employees under any circumstances.

If an employee experiences VPN connectivity issues, they should first restart their VPN client and check their internet connection before submitting a support case.

VPN access is automatically revoked upon employee termination or extended leave exceeding 90 days.
